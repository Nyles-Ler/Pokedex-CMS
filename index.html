Welcome to Kowski Komics, Winnipeg’s friendly neighbourhood comic shop and a fun destination for Pokémon fans of all ages. From comics and collectibles to Pokédex favourites, we have something for every trainer.

Welcome to our homepage!

Kowski Komics is a local comic and collectibles shop proudly serving the Winnipeg community.

We offer comics, Pokémon merchandise, and a welcoming space for fans to explore, learn, and connect. Browse our site to discover featured Pokémon, learn about different types, and find out what is happening at the shop.

Pikachu is an Electric-type Pokémon known for storing electricity in the pouches on its cheeks. When excited or threatened, it can release powerful electric attacks in a flash.

Kowski Komics was founded in 2010 with a passion for comics, collectibles, and the vibrant fan community that brings them to life. Proudly serving the Winnipeg area, our shop has become a welcoming space for fans of all ages to discover their favourite stories, explore the world of Pokémon, and connect with others who share the same interests. Whether you are a longtime collector or just getting started, Kowski Komics is here to celebrate fandom and provide a fun, inclusive experience for everyone.
